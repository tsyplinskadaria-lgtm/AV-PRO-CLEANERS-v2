Frequently Asked Questions

Will I really notice a difference after the cleaning?

Absolutely! Most of our customers are amazed at how fresh, vibrant, and revived their carpets and upholstery look — many say it feels like brand new again!

What Type of Products Are Used for Cleaning?

We use professional-grade, eco-friendly cleaning solutions that are tough on dirt and stains but completely safe for kids, pets, and your whole family. Our products deliver deep, effective cleaning without leaving behind harsh chemicals or odors.

How long does it take for everything to dry?

Most carpets and upholstery are dry within a few hours, depending on ventilation and fabric type. We’ll always give you specific advice to speed up the drying safely!

What if I’m not happy with the results?

Your satisfaction is 100% guaranteed. If something isn’t right, just let us know — we’ll make it right because we truly care about your experience.

Do you offer bundle pricing if I have multiple items or rooms to clean?

Yes! When you book multiple services together, we provide special bundled rates to make it even more affordable to refresh your whole space.

How soon can I schedule an appointment?

We offer flexible scheduling — often with same-week availability! Just reach out, and we’ll find a time that works perfectly for you.

How long will my carpets and upholstery stay clean after the service?

It depends on the type of fabric and the level of daily use, but with our deep cleaning methods and professional-grade products, most customers enjoy noticeably fresher, cleaner carpets and upholstery for months. We also provide personalized recommendations on how often to clean based on your specific items and how they’re used, helping you keep everything looking its best even longer!
