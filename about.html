About Us

Our Mission & Values

At Cleaning Llamas, our mission is simple:

Deliver real results and make your home feel clean again — not just look clean.

We’re a local, family-focused carpet and upholstery cleaning company serving Chicago and the surrounding suburbs. Every job we take on is personal to us, because we know you’re trusting us with your home, your furniture, and your comfort.

Friendly and Professional Service

From the first booking and beyond, we build lasting relationships with our customers.

When you book with Cleaning Llamas, you’re working with a small, dedicated local team that truly cares about the outcome of every job.

We show up:

  • On time
  • Prepared
  • Ready to do the job right

And we treat your home the same way we would treat our own.

Results-Focused Cleaning

Anyone can run a machine over a couch or carpet. What makes the difference is attention to detail and stain treatment expertise.

We specialize in:

  • Ink and marker stain removal
  • Pet stains and odor treatment
  • Heavy buildup from kids, pets, and daily use

Not every stain is guaranteed — and we’ll always be honest about that. But we put in the time, effort, and technique to get the best possible result every time.

A Better, Safer Clean

We use professional-grade equipment and safe, effective cleaning solutions that are suitable for homes with:

  • Kids
  • Pets
  • Allergies

Our process focuses on deep cleaning + proper extraction, so there’s no sticky residue left behind — just clean, fresh fabric.

Customer-First Approach

We built Cleaning Llamas around one idea: treat customers the way we’d want to be treated.

That means:

  • Clear communication
  • No hidden pricing
  • Honest recommendations
  • No upselling things you don’t need

If something isn’t realistic, we’ll tell you. If something can be improved — we’ll go all in.
